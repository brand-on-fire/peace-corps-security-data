# Data attribution and limits

Earthquakes: U.S. Geological Survey Earthquake Hazards Program, https://earthquake.usgs.gov/ . Epicenters are occurrence points, not local shaking footprints. Sources and provider update times are preserved.

Hazards: Global Disaster Alert and Coordination System, https://www.gdacs.org/ . Provider alert classifications do not establish Volunteer exposure.

Organized violence baseline: Uppsala Conflict Data Program, GED 26.1 and Candidate datasets, https://ucdp.uu.se/downloads/ . UCDP data are CC BY 4.0; cite Davies, Pettersson and Oberg (2026), https://doi.org/10.1093/jopres/xjag046, Sundberg and Melander (2013), and Hegre et al. (2020) for Candidate data. Full original source citations and precision fields remain with each record. Candidate coverage ends August 2026 in the initial baseline. This workflow does not infer later conflict events from missing data.

Country outlines: Natural Earth, public domain, https://www.naturalearthdata.com/ . Administrative outlines: geoBoundaries (William & Mary geoLab), https://www.geoboundaries.org/ . Each copied map layer records its source URL, year and license. Derived area assignments are spatial inferences using the provider's simplified geometry, not official site assessments.

Country presence evidence: Peace Corps official country pages and FY2027 Congressional Budget Justification, https://files.peacecorps.gov/documents/Peace_Corps_FY_2027_CBJ.pdf . Country rows preserve their exact evidence URLs and review dates.

Local news remains the publisher's work. The dataset retains attributed titles, links, timestamps, hashes and reviewed factual summaries, and does not republish full article/feed bodies. Distinct publications have not been established as editorially independent. This dataset is not a substitute for Peace Corps instructions or local emergency information.
